# Support

Shouldn't Wouldn't is a free local Chrome extension. Think Twice Before You Share.

Contact: support@shouldntwouldnt.com

This note does not promise a response time, ongoing updates, or perpetual support.

The extension is completely free. There is no purchase, subscription, license key, or upgrade, so there is nothing to buy, restore, or refund.

What the extension can and cannot do:

- It shows a reminder from field labels and a few local page signals. It does not read what you type.
- For Social Security, card, bank, routing, government ID, passport, and driver's license numbers, it shows dots instead of the characters. Press Show in the note to see them. If a site does not work with the dots, press Show or Dismiss for that field.
- A "!" on the toolbar button means the page removed or covered a reminder.
- It does not say a website is safe or malicious.
- "Unable to determine" means the extension is off, or it cannot run on that page.
- Turning protection off is saved in this browser profile. It stays off across refreshes, new pages, and browser restarts until you turn it back on.
- A website can still hide, cover, or remove a reminder. The extension cannot stop a site that is trying to do that.

For a hands-on check, use the steps in `store/install.md` and the fake pages under `fixtures/`. The manual cases are listed in `store/manual-tests.md`.
